## LS-203 · Sign in with Zalo (phase 2)

**User story**
As a Vietnamese guest,
I want to sign in with Zalo,
so that I can see my booking without creating a password.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AC1 Given I'm on the booking page, When I click "Continue with Zalo" and approve,
      Then I see "Signed in as <my Zalo name>".
- AC2 Given I cancel on the Zalo screen, When I'm sent back,
      Then the booking page is unchanged and no error is shown.
- AC3 Given I'm signed in, When I click "Sign out",
      Then I'm signed out and the button shows "Continue with Zalo" again.

**Scope**: sign in and sign out only.
**Non-scope**: Google or email sign-in, booking history, sharing to Zalo.
