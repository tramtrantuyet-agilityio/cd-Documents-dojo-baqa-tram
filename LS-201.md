## LS-201 · Airport pickup add-on

**User story**
As a guest arriving at Da Nang airport,
I want to add an airport pickup when I check the price,
so that I know the total cost and have a ride waiting.

**Acceptance criteria**
- AC1 Given any valid booking, When I tick "Airport pickup" and enter a flight number,
      Then 350,000 VND is added to the total and shown on its own line.
- AC2 Given "Airport pickup" is ticked, When I leave the flight number empty,
      Then I see "Please enter your flight number." and no total.
- AC3 Given "Airport pickup" is not ticked, When I check the price,
      Then no pickup line appears.

**Scope**: pickup from Da Nang airport, one way, price shown at booking.
**Non-scope**: drop-off to the airport, other airports, driver assignment, payment.

**Open questions** (for the client)
1. Is 350,000 VND per car (up to 4 guests) or per person? (price assumed)
2. Is there a night surcharge after 22:00?
3. How many hours before arrival must guests book the pickup?
